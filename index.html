About Me

I am a Full Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, where I direct the Robotics Laboratory.[cite: 1, 2] My extensive academic and applied track record specializes in robotic grasp planning, objects manipulation, and motion planning.[cite: 1] By developing "purpose-built" systems, my research bridges mathematical principles with profound real-world impacts spanning warehouse automation, defense technologies, and medical devices.[cite: 1]

Research Highlights

Licensed Healthcare Solution

The BaMper System

Co-developed a unique balance measurement and dynamic perturbation training trainer to evaluate and improve reactive recovery in elderly subjects, lowering global falling hazards. Successfully commercialized via industrial licensing.[cite: 1]

General Motors Initiative
