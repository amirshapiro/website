About Me

I am a Full Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, where I direct the Robotics Laboratory. My extensive academic and applied track record specializes in robotic grasp planning, objects manipulation, and motion planning. Beyond industrial automation, I seek to prevent falls of humans and the elderly, and develop rehabilitation devices to train people improve their balance.
